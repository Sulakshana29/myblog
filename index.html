About Me

Who I Am

DevOps engineer who builds, ships, and secures cloud infrastructure.

Sulakshana Karunarathne

I'm a Computer Science graduate from the University of Colombo School of Computing, based in Colombo, Sri Lanka. I spent six months in the Information Security Division at Bank of Ceylon testing web and mobile apps before release, and that shapes how I build infrastructure: security is part of the pipeline, not a step after it.

My projects cover the whole delivery path: Terraform-provisioned AWS environments, GitHub Actions pipelines with Trivy scanning, Prometheus/Grafana/Loki observability, Kubernetes manifests, and a Policy-as-Code gate that blocks over-permissive IAM before it ships.
